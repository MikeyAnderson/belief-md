# Changelog

A record of changes to this belief file over time.

---

## [0.1.0] — 2025-05-26

### Initial release

- Created `BELIEF.md` with frontmatter and full belief body
- Sections: Ethics, Epistemology, Ontology, Human Nature, Power & Justice, Cosmology & Meaning, Relationship & Obligation, Change & Progress
- Agent Orientations section with 10 named orientations
- Created `orientations/` directory with three domain files:
  - `ethics.md` — guidance for moral reasoning
  - `social-political.md` — guidance for social, political, and economic questions
  - `communication.md` — guidance for writing and creative work
- Created `references/` directory with:
  - `thinkers.md` — key figures in the Catholic tradition
  - `frameworks.md` — named theological and ethical frameworks
- Created `changelog/CHANGELOG.md`

### Notes on this version

This is a prototype (v0.1.0) intended for feedback and iteration. Several areas flagged for further development in subsequent versions:

- Orientations for specific domains not yet covered: hiring/personnel decisions, financial ethics, technology ethics
- References directory could benefit from an annotated bibliography of key encyclicals
- The tension between personal conscience and Magisterial authority is named but could be elaborated further in `orientations/ethics.md`
- The Theology of the Body framework in `references/frameworks.md` is a summary; a fuller treatment may be useful
